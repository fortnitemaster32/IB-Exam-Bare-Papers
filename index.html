Compatibility & filename detection

IB Exam Bare Papers works with any IB exam PDF — Mathematics, Sciences, Language & Literature, Individuals & Societies, Language Acquisition, and The Arts. It detects and strips copyright pages, instruction sheets, and blank pages, then labels the first page with the exam metadata.

Supported filename patterns

The tool parses exam metadata (subject, paper number, timezone, level, year, language) from your filenames. Below are the recognised patterns:

2024_Mathematics_analysis_and_approaches_paper_3__TZ2_HL.pdf Subject: AA · Paper 3 · TZ2 · HL · Year: 2024
Biology_paper_1__TZ2_HL.pdf Subject: Biology · Paper 1 · TZ2 · HL

What gets skipped

Files matching these patterns are automatically skipped:

  • Markschemes — filenames containing markscheme
  • Booklets — question_booklet, resource_booklet, text_booklet
  • Listening comprehensions — listening_comprehension
  • No paper number — filenames without paper_N or short format (P1 Zone A)

How question detection works

For each page, the tool extracts the text and checks for question indicators:

  • Numbered items like 1. or 12.
  • Maximum mark brackets like [Maximum mark: X]
  • Action verbs like find, show, calculate, determine, discuss, explain, describe, evaluate, identify, justify, outline, analyse, sketch, prove, solve, state, hence, compare, contrast, to what extent
  • Letter-coded sub-questions like (a), (b) or multiple-choice A., B.

Pages identified as copyright, instructions, or blank (under 30 characters) are automatically removed regardless.

Year detection

If your filenames include a year (e.g. 2024_...), it is extracted automatically. Otherwise, you can enter the year manually in the settings above — this is optional and only affects the output filename.

Need to report an issue?

If your PDF uses a naming convention not listed here, open an issue on GitHub with your filename and I may add support.

All processing happens in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
